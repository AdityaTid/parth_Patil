Safety First

Zero Compromise on Safety

Safety is not a cost — it's a competitive advantage. Our certified management system, rigorous training culture, and real-time site monitoring have delivered a 5-year lost-time injury-free record.

  • ISO 45001:2018 Occupational Health & Safety Management System
  • CSCS, CPCS, and NPORS certified operators across the fleet
  • Full lift planning with LICM-accredited Appointed Person on every project
  • Ground-bearing analysis and outrigger load calculations for every mobilisation
  • Real-time LMI monitoring with automatic overload cut-off on all machines
  • Emergency response protocol with 24/7 on-call engineering support
